# Privacy Policy

_Rewind for Facebook. Last updated: September 24, 2026._

Rewind does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data. It has no server, no analytics
and no tracking.

## What Rewind accesses

Rewind runs only on `facebook.com`, and only in your browser.

- **Page content.** When you start a search, Rewind reads the page you are viewing: the dates,
  text and links of its posts and reels. It shows them in its panel.
- **Your Facebook session.** To read a page, Rewind sends requests to Facebook the same way
  Facebook's own page does, using your existing login. It never sees or stores your password.

These requests go only to Facebook. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## What Rewind stores

- **Your panel settings** (posts or reels, how many, and the start date) are saved in your
  browser's local storage for `facebook.com`, so they are remembered next time.
- **Search results** are kept in memory only while the tab is open. They are gone when you
  reload or close the tab.

Results leave the panel only when you choose to: **Copy links** copies them to your clipboard,
and **Download CSV** saves a file on your computer.

## Permissions

Rewind asks for no browser permissions beyond running on `facebook.com` pages.
